Privacy Policy

Personal Workspace Automation · Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Personal Workspace Automation ("the app") accesses, uses, stores and shares data obtained through Google APIs. The app is a personal, single-user tool operated by its owner on the owner's own computer. It is not offered as a public service.

1. What data the app accesses

After the owner grants consent on Google's OAuth screen, the app may access the following data belonging to the owner:

  • Google Drive — files and folders created or used by the app, to locate and store the owner's ledger and notes
  • Google Sheets — the owner's personal ledger spreadsheet, to append transactions and read summaries
  • Google Docs — documents used for notes and generated summaries
  • Google Calendar — the owner's calendars, to create, read and update personal events

2. How the app uses data

Data is used only to perform operations that the owner explicitly requests. Typical operations are appending an expense row to a ledger, producing a monthly summary, and creating a bill-due event in a calendar. No data is used for profiling, advertising, marketing, or any secondary purpose.

3. Storage and retention

All processing takes place locally on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in an encrypted local credential store on that machine. There is no application server and no cloud database operated by this app. Data is retained only as long as it exists in the owner's own Google account or local files, and the owner controls deletion of both.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party. Data is not transferred to advertisers, data brokers, or AI model training pipelines. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized machine learning models.

Limited Use. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Security

Access is protected by Google OAuth 2.0. Credentials are held locally and encrypted at rest. Only the owner, who is the sole user, has access to the machine and credentials. The app requests the minimum scopes required for the operations above.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

The owner may revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately invalidates the stored tokens; local credential files can then be deleted from the machine. Deleting the ledger spreadsheet, documents or calendar events in Google removes the corresponding data permanently.

7. Children

The app is not directed at anyone under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
